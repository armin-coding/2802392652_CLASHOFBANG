About Clash of BaNG

Discover the origins of the greatest Dark Troop community.

Our History & How We Started

Originally started as a small online forum in 2018, Clash of BaNG was built by a handful of players who were obsessed with mastering Dark Elixir strategies. What began as a place to share P.E.K.K.A deployment tips quickly expanded into three massive regional community hubs located in Jakarta, Bandung, and Sanur.

Key Milestones

  • 2018: The original Clash of BaNG forum goes live.
  • 2020: Reached 10,000 active members across Indonesia.
  • 2023: Hosted the first tri-city friendly war tournament.
  • 2026: Launch of this official global website!

The Creators

Our founding team consists of three veteran Clan Leaders who wanted to create a non-toxic, highly strategic environment for players to learn. We are dedicated to providing high-quality troop guides, hero strategies, and a space for amazing fan art.
